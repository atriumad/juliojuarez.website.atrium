## Design Context

### Users
Prospective private-dining clients in Kansas City: people planning an intimate occasion (anniversary, milestone dinner, small gathering) who want a chef to cook for them at a single table. They arrive by referral or search, mostly on phones, and judge trust and taste in seconds. The job to be done: decide this chef is the right one and send an inquiry. Booking is by inquiry only; there is no checkout.

Client: Chef Julio Juarez. Eighteen years leading Kansas City kitchens (Starkers, The Gallery Event Space, JJ's Restaurant, Prime Social, Ocean Prime). This is his new private dining concept.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **restrained, exacting, quiet**. Fine, luxury, clean. Restraint over flash. The food "doesn't need to shout", so the site doesn't either. Voice is first-person, plain, confident, never salesy. Emotional goal: calm confidence and the sense of being personally looked after.

Identity: name-led. Wordmark "Julio Juarez" in a refined high-contrast serif, "Private Dining" as a small, widely tracked uppercase sans descriptor. Interlocked JJ monogram for small formats only (favicon, stamp). No icons, chef hats, forks/knives, flourishes, or script overload.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial and quiet. Near-white ground and near-black ink that read as true black-and-white, with only a faint sepia cast (neutral chroma <= 0.008) like a toned print. A single muted sepia accent used sparingly, and only one accent anywhere in the system. No earth tones. Moody black-and-white photography, generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts in hero and About, left-aligned body copy. Philosophy section inverts to a dark background as a single deliberate moment.

References: NOWA, ELENO, Ariel Mourelle, Montis case-stories layout.

NOWA (screenshot supplied by the client's team) is the north star for feel. What we take from it:
- Photo sits in an inset frame on the near-white page (margin all round, soft ~1rem corners, no shadow), not edge-to-edge.
- Photography is black-and-white with a light sepia tone, never saturated or earthy. One shared grade (`photo-grade`) for every image.
- Tiny widely tracked uppercase labels; body copy light and small; wordmark small and calm.
- Secondary actions are text links with a fine sepia underline, not filled buttons.
- Contact block is label / value rows (label left in muted caps, value right), separated by dotted rules, on a near-black panel beside a portrait.
- A fine line-art monogram seal sits in a photo corner (our JJ monogram, once its direction is decided).
What we do NOT copy: NOWA's light geometric sans wordmark. Ours is the high-contrast serif, per the identity brief.

Anti-references: terracotta and any earth-tone palette (brown, bronze, copper, tan); acid-green on near-black; generic SaaS card kits (identical rounded cards with soft grey shadows); restaurant-generic imagery and icons; scattered fade-and-slide-up entrances; numbered 01/02/03 sections (the career timeline may show years as labels because it is a real sequence).

Type: two families maximum, clearly distinct. **Prata** (display, weight 400 only, no italic) + **Urbanist** (body and all tracked-caps labels, variable weight). Chosen by the client from a 12-pairing comparison. Rules: Prata only for headlines and the wordmark (>= ~28px), never for body or labels; no display italic and no display bold (`font-synthesis: none`), so the Philosophy quote is upright and "Private Dining" under the name is Urbanist tracked caps; everything under ~24px is Urbanist.

Theme: light by default (near-white), with one dark section (Philosophy). No full dark mode.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint is the luxury.** Every element earns its place. Whitespace and typography carry the page, not decoration.
2. **One accent, used rarely.** Sepia appears only where it directs attention (primary action, a hairline, a hover state). If sepia is everywhere, it is nowhere.
3. **Photography leads, layout stays out of its way.** Monochrome, moody images; layouts are editorial and asymmetric, never a repeated card grid.
4. **Motion is quiet.** Smooth scroll and simple hover states only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No entrance animations on every section.
5. **Book a Dinner is always one tap away.** Pinned in the nav on desktop and mobile, repeated in the footer. Mobile is a first-class layout with safe-area insets respected.
6. **Accessible by default.** Visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast (verify sepia on near-white), reduced-motion support, semantic markup (timeline as a real table).

### Constraints
Layout: the content container spans the full viewport width (no max-width) with a single screen-proportional gutter (`--gutter`, 16px phone to 64px cap) used for the sides AND, on the framed hero, top and bottom, so the page margin around the photo frame is equal on all four sides. Text measure is capped per element in `ch`, not by the container.
Single page, six sections plus footer, anchor navigation only. Copy is in English and final-draft. Placeholder photography and dishes will be swapped for real content, so imagery uses fixed aspect ratios and content lives in a data file.
